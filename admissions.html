Admissions Information

Admission Guidelines for New Students

Hosanna Secondary School welcomes applications from students who are eager to learn and contribute to our school community. Admission is based on merit, considering previous academic records and an entry assessment (where applicable).

Application Process:

  1. Obtain an application form from the school's admission office or download it from our website.
  2. Complete the application form accurately and attach all required documents (e.g., previous school report cards, birth certificate copy).
  3. Submit the completed form and required documents to the admissions office by the specified deadline.
  4. Shortlisted candidates may be invited for an interview or entrance examination.
  5. Successful applicants will receive an admission offer letter with details on enrollment.

Enrollment Procedures

Once a student has been offered admission, the following steps are typically required for enrollment:

  • Acceptance of the admission offer.
  • Payment of the required school fees by the specified deadline.
  • Submission of any outstanding documents.
  • Purchase of school uniform and other required scholastic materials.
  • Attendance of the orientation program for new students (if applicable).
