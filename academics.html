Our Academic Programs

School Curriculum

Hosanna Secondary School follows the National Curriculum as prescribed by the Ministry of Education and Sports. Our curriculum is designed to be comprehensive, balanced, and challenging, aiming to equip students with the knowledge and skills necessary for success in higher education and beyond.

We offer a wide range of subjects at both O-Level (Uganda Certificate of Education - UCE) and A-Level (Uganda Advanced Certificate of Education - UACE).

Academic Departments

Sciences Department

Subjects offered: Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Agriculture.

Mathematics Department

Subjects offered: Mathematics (Compulsory at O-Level, Principal and Subsidiary at A-Level).

Languages Department

Subjects offered: English Language, Literature in English, Kiswahili, Luganda.

Humanities Department

Subjects offered: History, Geography, Economics, Divinity (CRE).

Vocational Studies & Arts Department

Subjects offered: Fine Art, Technical Drawing, Entrepreneurship Education, Computer Studies.

Co-curricular Activities

We believe in holistic development. Our co-curricular activities provide students with opportunities to explore their talents, develop leadership skills, and maintain a healthy lifestyle.

Sports & Games

Football, Netball, Volleyball, Basketball, Athletics, Table Tennis.

Clubs & Societies

Debate Club, Writers' Club, Science Club, Scripture Union, Drama Club, Music Club, Red Cross Society, Scouting.

MDD (Music, Dance, and Drama)

Students actively participate in inter-school and intra-school MDD competitions.
